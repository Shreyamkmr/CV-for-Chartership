**Not for submission or external sharing.** This is a scratch file to help
draft the actual UK-SPEC competence report and prep for referee
conversations / interview. It records the specific internal evidence you
said you found, so you can go back to source documents and confirm exact
dates, your personal contribution, and any measurable outcome.

Everything below is **unverified** — it's exactly what you reported finding,
not something this session checked. Before it's used for anything real:
- Confirm each item against the actual source document/meeting.
- Separate what *you* did from what the wider team/other individuals did —
  UK-SPEC assessors want personal contribution, not programme description.
- Check with EET Fuels' confidentiality/IP policy before quoting internal
  document titles, system names, or colleagues' names in anything that
  leaves the company (a referee report reviewed by IOM3 is usually fine;
  a CV handed to a third party generally is not).

## Open questions for you

1. On each programme above, what did *you* specifically decide, design, or
   own — vs. coordinate, support, or attend meetings on? Assessors weight
   this heavily.
2. Any measurable outcomes for each (cost saved, time saved, downtime
   avoided, number of users/sites, error rate improvement)?
3. Which UK-SPEC route are you going for (Standard / Further Learning /
   Experiential Learning)? This affects how much emphasis goes on
   underpinning knowledge (A) vs. demonstrated competence (B-E).
4. IOM3 membership status/grade/number, and who your two referees will be.
5. CPD — what have you actually done (courses, internal training, technical
   reading, conferences, mentoring) since qualifying?
